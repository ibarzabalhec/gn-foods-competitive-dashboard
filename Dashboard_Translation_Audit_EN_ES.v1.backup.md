## Translation conventions (locked)

- **Brand names** (Tommy Burritos, Chick'n Spot, Tommy Beans, Barrio Chick'en, Taco Bell, Chipotle, Raising Cane's, KFC, Wingstop, Chick-fil-A, Church's, Popeyes, McDonald's, Starbucks, Sweetgreen, Qdoba, Del Taco, Culver's, Slim Chickens, Huey Magoo's, Bojangles, Golden Chick, Five Guys, Panda Express, Ruby Tuesday, TGI Fridays, Dave's Hot Chicken, etc.): kept verbatim.
- **US place names** (Texas, Florida, Houston, Miami, Dallas-Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville, Brickell, etc.): kept verbatim.
- **Industry acronyms** (QSR, AUV, CAGR, COGS, KPI, IFA, NRN, NRA, BLS, DOL, SKU, P&L, POS, AI, LTO, RE, TX, FL, US, YoY, GA, SC, NC, VA, AZ, FY): kept verbatim.
- **Currency:** US dollar amounts kept verbatim ($761K, $1.02M, $63.7B). Do NOT convert to pesos.
- **Numbers:** kept in US format. "5.6%" stays "5.6%", "4,051" stays "4,051".
- **Drive-thru:** kept verbatim (universal industry term).
- **In-Line / Standalone:** kept verbatim as format-class labels.
- **Master franchise:** "franquicia maestra".
- **Tex-Mex:** kept verbatim.
- **Franchisee → franquiciatario; Franchisor → franquiciante; Royalty → regalía.**
- **Multi-unit operator:** "operador multi-unidad".
- **Suburban / urban:** "suburbano / urbano".
- **Right-to-work state:** "estado de derecho al trabajo (right-to-work)".
